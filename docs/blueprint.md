# **App Name**: EcoCatalyst Hub

## Core Features:

- Landing Page & Program Showcase: A captivating landing page featuring 'Tunong Baroh' profile, program highlights, past activities documentation, and clear calls-to-action for booking and registration.
- Program Catalog: Browse detailed program listings categorized into Outdoor Training, Indoor Training, Nature School, and Reforestation/Mangrove programs, complete with descriptions, duration, location, pricing, and expected outcomes.
- Booking System: A core booking mechanism allowing institutions and schools to select programs, specify participant numbers, and choose desired dates. This system will manage booking statuses and store booking data in the PostgreSQL database.
- Environmental Impact Programs: Enable users to participate in specific environmental initiatives such as Mangrove Planting (selecting number of seedlings, location) and Seedling Purchase (choosing plant types, quantity), with details stored in the PostgreSQL database.
- Admin Dashboard: A centralized administration panel to manage program offerings, track and process bookings, oversee user accounts, and generate activity reports, with all data interactions handled via the PostgreSQL database.
- Activity Gallery & Testimonials: Showcase visual documentation of past activities through photo and video galleries, alongside testimonials from previous participants to build trust and highlight program impact.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: A sophisticated, earthy green (#459967) that embodies growth, nature, and reliability. This will be a cornerstone of the branding, symbolizing the core mission of environmental education and sustainability.
- Background color: A serene, very light green (#EDF7F0), providing a clean and inviting canvas that reflects the calm of natural environments while ensuring readability and a fresh feel.
- Accent color: A vibrant, energetic yellow-green (#B3F22E) used sparingly to highlight calls-to-action, key information, and interactive elements, adding a lively and inspiring touch.
- Headline font: 'Belleza', a humanist sans-serif, brings a sense of refined artistry and contemporary design to titles, aligning with the visual appeal of art and nature-focused content.
- Body font: 'Alegreya', a humanist serif, provides an elegant, intellectual, and highly readable experience for longer blocks of text, making educational content engaging and pleasant to consume.
- Utilize icons that are simple, modern, and directly relate to nature, education, and outdoor activities (e.g., leaves, mountains, compass, open books), rendered with a clean line-art style.
- Implement a modular, responsive layout that prioritizes clear information hierarchy and intuitive navigation, featuring prominent call-to-action buttons. The design will be fully mobile-friendly to ensure accessibility on various devices.
- Subtle, fluid animations on interactive elements (e.g., button hovers, section transitions) and loading states to enhance user engagement and provide a polished, dynamic experience without being distracting.
